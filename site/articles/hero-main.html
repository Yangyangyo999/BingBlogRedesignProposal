How to prepare your site for better visibility in Bing Search

By Alex Morgan Jordan Taylor Casey Bennett Aug 12, 2024 7 min read
Learn the essential steps to optimize your pages for Bing Search, including structured data, page experience, metadata and content clarity.

Key Takeaways

  • Use accurate structured data to help Bing understand the intent and format of each page.
  • Prioritize fast loading, mobile-friendly layouts, and secure connections.
  • Write clear metadata and page content that directly answers the user's search intent.
  • Review search analytics after launch and refine content based on real query performance.

Bing evaluates your content through a combination of technical signals and relevance. Preparing your site for search visibility means making it easy for crawlers to understand your pages and for users to find the answers they need.

Structured data helps Bing understand intent

Rich markup like JSON-LD gives explicit context to your content. Use schema types that match your article, product, FAQ, or event so Bing can present richer search experiences.

<script type="application/ld+json"> { "@context": "https://schema.org", "@type": "Article", "headline": "How to prepare your site for better visibility in Bing Search", "author": { "@type": "Person", "name": "Alex Morgan" } } </script>

Make your page experience consistent

Page speed, mobile friendliness, and secure connections all matter. A clean page layout with fast-loading assets reduces friction and improves your chances in search results.

After launch, measure performance using search analytics and adjust content based on queries and page engagement.

Frequent Asked Questions

What is the first step to improve visibility in Bing Search?

Start by making sure Bing can crawl and index your important pages, then confirm that each page has a clear purpose, descriptive metadata, and useful content.

Is structured data required for ranking?

Structured data is not required, but accurate markup helps Bing understand your content and can make pages eligible for richer search experiences.

How important is mobile page experience?

A consistent mobile experience is essential because it helps visitors reach content quickly and interact with the page without unnecessary friction.

How often should search performance be reviewed?

Review performance regularly after publishing and after significant site changes. Use query and engagement trends to guide meaningful updates.
