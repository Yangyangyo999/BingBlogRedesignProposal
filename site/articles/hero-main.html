Introducing Topics Portfolio (Beta) in AI Performance

By Krishna Saral Vivek 6 min read
New beta feature in Bing Webmaster Tools that shows the thematic shape of your site’s presence in AI Search

Key Takeaways

  • See your AI Search presence grouped into personalized thematic clusters.
  • Compare Topic Size, Sites Cited and Queries to understand demand and competition.
  • Explore opportunity queries where other sites appear and yours does not.
  • Treat beta metrics as directional signals and use feedback controls to improve the view.

Today we are announcing a new beta feature inside the AI Performance report in Bing Webmaster Tools. It is called Topics Portfolio, and it groups the grounding queries where your site appears into broader thematic clusters – for example, football, taxes or AI Chatbots – so you can see the shape of your presence across those clusters rather than one query at a time.

AI Performance gives you a direct view into how your site contributes to AI-generated answers – from the grounding queries where it appears to the pages cited for each query. Topics Portfolio adds the broader view – how that presence is distributed across themes, how much demand each theme represents, how competitive each theme is and where your site has room to grow.

Everything in this view is personalized to your site. The topics and the opportunity queries are surfaced from your own presence and activity in AI Search, not from a generic ecosystem list.

Topics Portfolio

Login to Bing Webmaster Tools and navigate to AI Performance. If there is sufficient data for your site, you will see a new Topics Portfolio tab next to Citations & Pages. The landing page opens to your topic portfolio: a personalized list of the topics where your site has a meaningful presence, with three ecosystem measures beside each one.

Here is what each column means.

ColumnWhat it tells you
TopicA broad thematic cluster of related grounding queries where your site appears.
Topic SizeThe approximate total citations across all sites for this topic over the selected period – a measure of how much AI Search demand the topic represents.
Sites CitedThe approximate number of sites appearing for queries in this topic, which indicates how crowded the topic is.
QueriesThe approximate number of distinct grounding queries in this topic.

Below the topic table you will find Top Opportunity Queries: the strongest queries across your whole portfolio where other sites appear and yours does not. It is a fast, cross-topic read on where you could grow. Citation volumes there are shown as ranges rather than exact numbers.

Inside a topic

Click any topic and you drop into a detail page for it. Three KPI cards sit at the top – the Topic Size, your own citations within the topic, and the number of queries in it – and beneath them the queries themselves are split into two tables.

Your Grounding Queries lists the top queries in this topic where your site already appears, aligned with the data you already know from Citations & Pages. Opportunity Queries lists the top queries in the same topic where other sites appear and yours does not, with citation volumes shown as ranges. Together they give you the two halves of a topic: what you already own, and what is still open.

How Topics Portfolio helps

Topics Portfolio is aimed at anyone who wants to understand and grow their site’s presence in AI-generated answers, and it should be most useful to publishers doing content strategy, editorial planning and competitive positioning. It gives you three things the report could not show before:

  • A portfolio view – the thematic shape of your AI Search footprint at a glance.
  • Ecosystem context – how big each topic is, and how crowded it is with other sites.
  • Actionable opportunity – specific queries within specific topics where you can grow, drawn from real grounding activity happening on Bing.

This is a beta feature – here is what that means

We are shipping Topics Portfolio with a beta label. The view is built on AI-generated classification and on ecosystem aggregates, and we would rather put it in front of you early and improve it with your input than hold it back. Treat the numbers as directional signals for planning, not as a precise ledger. A few limitations are worth knowing before you start:

  • Topic classification is AI-generated and can occasionally be imperfect, so a query may land in a topic you would have labelled differently.
  • Topics with insufficient site presence or insufficient ecosystem activity are not shown, so smaller sites may see a shorter list or may not even see this feature.
  • Branded queries can appear as opportunity queries. In a Running Shoes topic, for instance, contososhoes.com may see Air Shoes listed as an opportunity even though it is a Compete-branded query.
  • Topic Size, Sites Cited and Queries are approximate, and opportunity citation volumes are deliberately shown as ranges.

Tell us what you think

Feedback is built directly into the view, and it is the fastest way to make this better. Every topic row and every opportunity query carries a thumbs up and a thumbs down control – you can see them in both screenshots above. If a topic does not describe your content, or an opportunity query is off the mark for your site, mark it down. If a cluster nails the way you actually plan your coverage, mark it up. Those signals go straight to the team working on the classification and the opportunity selection, and they shape what we fix first.

You can also reach us through the usual Bing Webmaster Tools feedback channels to tell us about an issue in more detail than a thumbs up or thumbs down allows.

Getting started

Sign in to Bing Webmaster Tools, open the AI Performance report for your site, and select the Topics tab. Choose a one-, two- or three-month window, start with the topics where your citations are already concentrated, then work down the opportunity queries. And when something looks wrong, tell us with a thumb – that is what the beta label is for.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where can I find Topics Portfolio?

Sign in to Bing Webmaster Tools and open AI Performance. If your site has sufficient data, the Topics Portfolio tab appears next to Citations & Pages.

What are opportunity queries?

They are grounding queries where other sites appear and yours does not. Citation volumes are shown as ranges rather than exact numbers.

How precise are the beta metrics?

Topic Size, Sites Cited and Queries are approximate. Use them as directional planning signals, and remember that AI-generated topic classification can be imperfect.

How can I share feedback?

Use the thumbs up and thumbs down controls on topic rows and opportunity queries, or use the usual Bing Webmaster Tools feedback channels for more detail.
