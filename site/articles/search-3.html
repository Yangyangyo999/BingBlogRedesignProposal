Bing Image Creator adds GPT-40 alongside DALL·E3

By Bing Blog Team Dec 1, 2024 4 min read
Bing Image Creator now supports GPT-40 prompts alongside DALL·E3, giving creators more powerful image generation options.
AI Images Creator

With GPT-40 integration, Bing Image Creator can produce more nuanced and expressive images from text prompts. This update expands the creative possibilities for every user.

Choose the right model for your idea

DALL·E3 remains ideal for visual concept generation, while GPT-40 is tailored for rich prompt understanding and more detailed creativity.

Try advanced prompts

Use descriptive language, artistic styles, and contextual details to get the best results. Start with a clear subject, then add mood, color, and setting.

Creators are encouraged to experiment with both models to see which one fits the vision of their project.
