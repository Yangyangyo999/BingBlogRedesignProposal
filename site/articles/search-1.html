How does Bing use structured data?

By Search Quality Insights May 22, 2024 8 min read
Structured data helps Bing determine meaning, content type and the best way to present a page in search results.
Search Structured Data AI

Bing uses structured data as a signal to better understand the relationships in your content. This can unlock rich results, knowledge panels, and answer cards that are more helpful to users.

The role of schema markup

Schema tells Bing what the content is about. An Article schema is very different from a Product schema, and giving Bing the right type is critical.

<script type="application/ld+json"> { "@context": "https://schema.org", "@type": "Article", "headline": "How does Bing use structured data?", "author": { "@type": "Organization", "name": "Bing" } } </script>

Improve relevancy and experience

When Bing understands the content, it can match search intent more precisely and display the page in a richer format for users.

Always validate your markup and avoid over-marking content. Keep schema focused and accurate to preserve trust and ranking value.
