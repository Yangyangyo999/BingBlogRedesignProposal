Introducing the New Bing Places for Business

By Bing Places Team Sep 5, 2024 6 min read
Explore the new Bing Places experience designed to help business owners manage their local listings and reach customers across search and maps.
Maps Business Local

Bing Places now offers a streamlined dashboard for creating and editing business profiles. This helps local businesses stay visible in search, maps, and AI-generated recommendations.

Update your profile in minutes

Fill in your business name, hours, contact details, and categories. Verified businesses appear more prominently in local search and map results.

Keep customers informed

Use posts, photos, and frequently asked questions to tell shoppers what makes your business unique. Regular updates help search engines refresh your profile faster.

Monitor how customers discover your business and optimize your listing based on query patterns and local interest.
