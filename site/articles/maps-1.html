How to verify a local business on Bing Maps

By Bing Maps Team Jun 10, 2024 6 min read
A verified business profile helps customers trust your listing and improves how your business appears in local search results.
Maps Verification Local

Verification ensures that business owners have control over their listing details and that customers see accurate information. Bing Maps provides a clear flow to confirm ownership and manage your profile.

Step 1: Claim your business

Search for your business in Bing Places and request ownership. If the listing already exists, follow the prompts to verify your connection to the business.

Step 2: Choose a verification method

Verification can be completed by phone, email, mail, or instant verification in supported cases. Select the method that works best for your business.

Once verified, your business information becomes more authoritative and can appear with enhanced details in search and maps.
